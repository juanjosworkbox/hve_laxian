## Answers to Research Questions

### 1. Enemy shooting while in formation

**Finding: ENEMIES PRIMARILY SHOOT DURING DIVES, NOT IN FORMATION**

Evidence:
- Wikipedia: "The aliens will make a dive bomb towards the bottom of the screen while shooting projectiles"
- Yokoyama: "Galaxian is all about weaving your way through the gaps of the missiles that are fired at you"
- Sawano: "We programmed them to judge the player's movements and react accordingly, so it seemed that they had a will and personality of their own, the way they would individually divebomb the player's Galaxip"

The emphasis is on enemies shooting WHILE diving toward the player, not while sitting in formation. The "weaving through gaps" description implies the bullets come from diving enemies.

**However**, the exact behavior of formation shooting is not explicitly addressed in the sources. In Space Invaders (the game Galaxian was designed to improve upon), enemies DO shoot while in formation. Given that Galaxian was designed as "the post-Invaders game," it's likely enemies also fired from formation, but the primary shooting action came from diving enemies.

### 2. Enemy movement speed during dives

**Finding: DIVE SPEED vs FORMATION SPEED NOT EXPLICITLY DOCUMENTED**

Evidence:
- Sawano: "the way the aliens flew in curved lines" - describes dive movement
- Sawano: "Enemy movement will increase as the game progresses" - overall difficulty scaling
- Sawano: "In Galaxian, the number of enemies never changes. The difficulty from one stage to the next is almost imperceptible"

The sources confirm enemies move in "curved lines" during dives, but don't explicitly state whether dive speed is faster or slower than formation movement. However, Sawano's emphasis on "weaving through gaps" and the dramatic nature of dive bombing suggests dives are FAST and DANGEROUS, implying they move faster than the formation movement.

### 3. Enemy dive patterns

**Finding: ENEMIES MOVE IN CURVED PATHS DURING DIVES**

Evidence:
- Sawano (1985): "People talked a lot about the scrolling starfield background, and the way the aliens flew in curved lines"
- Sawano: "We programmed them to judge the player's movements and react accordingly"

This is the most definitive finding: enemies move in CURVED paths during dives, not straight lines. This was specifically called out as a notable feature of the game. The curved paths, combined with enemy AI that "judges the player's movements," suggest enemies may adjust their dive trajectory to try to hit the player.

### 4. Enemy formation behavior

**Finding: FORMATION MOVEMENT AND SHOOTING NOT FULLY DOCUMENTED**

Evidence:
- Wikipedia: "The enemies appear in formation towards the top of the screen"
- Sawano: "In Galaxian, the number of enemies never changes"
- Sawano: "Enemy movement will increase as the game progresses"

The sources confirm enemies appear in formation at the top of the screen, but don't explicitly detail:
- Whether the formation moves side-to-side (like Space Invaders)
- The shooting frequency of enemies in formation
- The exact formation pattern

### 5. Enemy types and behaviors

**Finding: TWO ENEMY TYPES WITH COLOR VARIATIONS**

Evidence:
- Sawano: "We wanted a game that would keep evolving infinitely, using only two enemy types (4 if you count the color changes)"
- Wikipedia: "The enemies appear in formation towards the top of the screen, with two escort ships, labeled the 'Galaxian Flagship' or 'Galboss'"
- Yokoyama: "Galaxian Flagships will make a dive bomb with two red escort ships; shooting all three of these will award the player bonus points"
- Yokoyama: "That was something we added later in the development process of Galaxian" - referring to flagship with red guards

The sources confirm:
- Two basic enemy types
- Four color variations (2 types x 2 colors each, or similar)
- Flagship/Galboss: Special enemy that dives with two red escort ships
- Bonus points for shooting the flagship and its escorts together

### 6. Enemy return behavior

**Finding: RETURN BEHAVIOR NOT DOCUMENTED**

The sources do not explicitly address how enemies return to formation after diving. This is a gap in the research.

5. **Dive speed**: Is the dive speed faster or slower than formation movement? What is the characteristic speed difference?
